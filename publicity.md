\# Publicity Plan



\## Promotion Strategies



\- Create digital posters to promote the Campus Connect event.

\- Share event announcements through student communication channels.

\- Put up approved posters on campus noticeboards.

\- Ask student clubs and societies to help spread the word.

\- Prepare short social media posts highlighting the event activities.

\- Include the event date, venue, and registration details in promotional materials once confirmed.



\## Publicity Timeline



\- Prepare the first poster draft after the event details are discussed.

\- Share promotional materials after the date and venue are confirmed.

\- Post reminders as the event approaches.

\- Share a final reminder with participants before the event.

\- Review student engagement and feedback after the event.



\## Questions for Discussion



\- Which communication channels are most effective for reaching students?

\- Who will approve the publicity materials before they are shared?

\- How far in advance should promotion begin?
