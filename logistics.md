\# Logistics



\## Venue and setup



\- Book the multipurpose hall for the afternoon, with a classroom nearby as backup in case of rain.

\- Arrange 10 tables and 80 chairs, set up in small group clusters.

\- Borrow a projector, microphone and speakers from the AV office.

\- Set up a registration table near the main entrance.



\## Supplies and food



\- Prepare name tags, markers and printed maps for the campus discovery challenge.

\- Order light refreshments and bottled water for about 80 students.

\- Provide bins and recycling bags, and assign two volunteers to clean up afterwards.



\## Assumptions and questions for discussion



\- We are assuming the event will be held indoors. Do we need an outdoor option?

\- What is our budget for food and supplies?
